Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Journali is a personal open-source project. It is designed to keep your writing on your own device.

Who we are

Journali is developed by an individual developer and published under the GNU General Public License v3.0. There is no company, no backend server, and no account system.

Contact: https://github.com/Chris-Mooney852/journali/issues

What is stored on your device

  • Journal entries — the text you write, its date, tags, target language, and proficiency level.
  • Analysis reports — the feedback returned by the AI provider you chose.
  • API keys — the key for the AI provider you configured, stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Widget snapshot — a small summary (streak count, daily prompt, one vocabulary word) shared with the Journali home-screen widget.

All of this lives in the app's own storage on your device.

What leaves your device

Journali has no server and sends nothing to the developer.

When you request AI feedback, the app sends your journal text, your target language and level, and your API key directly to the provider you selected:

Provider Endpoint
DeepSeek api.deepseek.com
OpenAI api.openai.com
Anthropic api.anthropic.com
Google Gemini generativelanguage.googleapis.com
Groq api.groq.com
Custom / Ollama the address you enter; defaults to localhost

Those providers handle the data under their own privacy policies. The developer of Journali never receives a copy.

Demo mode sends nothing anywhere and reads no API key.

What is not collected

Journali contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not request your location, contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifier.

Third-party purchases

AI providers may charge for API usage. Those arrangements are between you and the provider. Journali does not process payments and receives no billing information.

Children

Journali is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete All Journal Data removes every journal entry, analysis report, widget snapshot, and saved API key from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes this data.

Because nothing is stored on a developer server, deleting the app deletes everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date.
